# Category photos

The photos at the head of each category's listing (`public/images/categories/`),
one per top-level category; a subcategory shows its parent's. Named by the
category's slug, so a new category gets one by adding `<slug>.webp` - no code.

All are CC0 or public domain: free to use, change and sell, with no credit
required. Scaled to at most 1024px wide and saved as WebP.
